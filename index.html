Questions

The things buyers ask first.

How does the transfer actually happen?

Once escrow confirms your payment, you get the authorization code and the domain moves into your registrar account. A push between accounts at the same registrar finishes the same day; a transfer between different registrars usually takes five to seven days.

What counts as a realistic offer?

There’s no formula and no listed price. Offers are weighed on what the name is worth to your business. A considered number with a line about what you’re building gets a considered reply; automated three-figure blasts get archived.

Which payment methods work?

Anything Escrow.com accepts — wire transfer, credit card, and PayPal on smaller amounts. Who pays the escrow fee is part of the negotiation, not a fixed rule.

Will you do a payment plan?

Possibly, on a shorter schedule and with the domain held in escrow until the final payment clears. Say so in your message and we’ll work out the terms.

Is a .app any different to own?

It’s an ordinary gTLD — no eligibility rules, no paperwork, and it transfers between registrars exactly like a .com. The one difference is that browsers refuse it over plain HTTP, so whatever you host on it needs a valid certificate from day one. Every serious host issues those free and automatically.
